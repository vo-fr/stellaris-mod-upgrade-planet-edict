[h1]Upgrade Buildings — Stellaris 1.9.1[/h1]

[b]A small quality-of-life mod for Stellaris 1.9.1 (Boulle).[/b]

Tired of upgrading mines, power plants and farms one by one? Select a planet, open [b]Planetary Edicts[/b] and choose [b]Upgrade Buildings[/b]. The edict starts eligible upgrades on that planet using normal construction costs and build times.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Mines, power plants and hydroponic farms, including basic colony buildings
[*] All 15 upgrade transitions, up to tier V where allowed
[*] Technology requirements and capital restrictions respected
[*] Skips buildings already under construction
[*] Other buildings remain untouched
[*] Seven languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Brazilian Portuguese
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
1. Select one of your planets.
2. Open Planetary Edicts.
3. Choose Upgrade Buildings (or its translated title).

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[b]For Stellaris 1.9.1 only.[/b] Not intended for modern Stellaris versions. Disable older experimental versions of this mod to avoid conflicts. Back up important saves before adding mods.
